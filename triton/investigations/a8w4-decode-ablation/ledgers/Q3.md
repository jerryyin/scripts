## Parent → child ownership change

- Before Q3, the target grouped A8W4 decode kernel consumes externally materialized grouped-route metadata. It does not own construction of expert histograms, offsets, schedules, or stable route permutations.
- After Q3, grid program 0 owns all `G<=64` precomputed routes, filters invalid IDs, creates every metadata field, and copies ordered gates. No second launch, top-k computation, or M=1 specialization participates.
- The math primitive changes from no owned route materializer to integer histogram, associative prefix scans, equality-based stable ranks, and indexed stores. It introduces no floating-point reduction and no gate arithmetic.

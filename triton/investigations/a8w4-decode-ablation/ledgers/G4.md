## Parent → child ownership change

- Before: one `(expert-grouped M block, N tile)` program calls the general ragged/dense/K-split `compute_offsets` helper. The child loads exactly one selected-`BLOCK_M` packed schedule entry, unpacks the same two IDs, loads only the expert row start, forms `off_m = BLOCK_M * block_id`, and keeps the frozen K offset at zero through `pid_k * BLOCK_K`.
- Expert grouping, grid mapping, selected `BLOCK_M`, TDM descriptors, X/W/scale global-to-LDS traffic, scaled-WMMA primitive, FP32 accumulation and bias, activation/output conversion, gather/scatter behavior, and numerical boundary remain unchanged.
